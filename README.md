## What is Telehealth?

**Telehealth** is an open, geospatial decision platform that identifies **healthcare deserts** and evaluates **telehealth feasibility** by combining:

- Public health infrastructure data  
- Broadband & internet performance data  
- Optional AI-driven explainability  
- Optional voice-based conversational access  

---

## Features

### Core Intelligence

- **Healthcare Desert Detection** — Identifies regions with critical healthcare access gaps.  
- **Telehealth Feasibility Index (TFI)** — Ranks regions by medical need × internet readiness.  
- **Interactive Alaska-First Map** — High-performance geospatial visualization focused on Alaska.  
- **Explainable Analytics** — Transparent breakdown of why each region ranks high or low.  

---

### AI Chatbot and Voice Assistant

- **Text-Based AI Chatbot**
  - Ask questions in natural language:
  - Get region-wise explanations and score breakdowns.
  - Supports policy queries for NGOs and government planners.

- **Voice-Based Assistant**
  - Ask questions using voice.
  - Spoken responses for accessibility-first usage.
  - Designed for rural users, elderly users, and low-literacy environments.

- **Automatic Map Highlighting**
  - Regions are highlighted dynamically based on text or voice queries.

---

### Policy Simulation

- Simulate:
  - Adding new hospitals  
  - Improving internet bandwidth  
  - Deploying mobile clinics  
- Instantly view **updated telehealth feasibility**.

---

### Low-Bandwidth Rural Mode

- Chatbot-first navigation  
- Minimal UI for slow internet zones  
- Optimized for mobile village usage  

---

### Reporting & Export

- Download:
  - Region reports (PDF)  
  - Raw metrics (CSV)  
- Designed for policy briefs, NGO planning, and academic research
